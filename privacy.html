Privacy Policy

claude-drive — last updated 21 September 2026

Who this applies to

claude-drive is a private, single-user utility. Its only user is its owner, who is also the owner of the Google account it connects to. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the application requests access to Google Drive in order to upload files to, and download files from, the owner's own Drive. It accesses no other Google service and no other person's account.

What is stored

An OAuth token is stored on the owner's own private server so the application can reconnect without repeating sign-in. File contents are transferred directly between that server and Google Drive.

What is shared

Nothing. No data obtained through the Google APIs is sold, shared, transferred to any third party, or used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training any model. There is no third party involved.

Retention and deletion

Files remain in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be withdrawn at any time by the owner at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored token.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the account listed as the developer contact for this application.
